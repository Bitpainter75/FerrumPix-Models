TreeNet (RawForge)
==================

Files in this repository: treenet-v2.onnx (current), treenet-v1.onnx (kept
for older FerrumPix versions)

Origin
------
Denoising models of RawForge, a raw-image denoiser.

  Original work:  https://github.com/rymuelle/RawForge
  Source files:   from the release onnx_v1.0.0 of that repository
                  (published 2026-04-17):
                    ShadowWeightedL1_24_deep_500.onnx  ("heavy")
                    ShadowWeightedL1_super_light.onnx  ("super light")

treenet-v1.onnx is the heavy model alone.

treenet-v2.onnx runs both, one after the other, in one file: the heavy model
with the ISO of the picture, then the super light model on its result with
the smaller of that ISO and 3200. The heavy model leaves part of the noise
standing at high ISO; the second, small pass takes about half of what is left
above ISO 3600 and costs about a sixth of the time of the first. The two
graphs were joined unchanged: the second one's names got the prefix "zweit_",
its input is the first one's output, and its "cond" is the first one's "cond"
limited to at most 0.5 by one added Min node. The script is FerrumPix's
packaging/Diagnostics/TreeNetMessung/verbinden.py.

  Conversion:     done for FerrumPix in both files: every float16 tensor of
                  the published files (weights, constants, cast targets,
                  inputs and outputs) was widened to float32. No retraining,
                  no change to any weight beyond that widening.

Why the conversion: in half precision the published file returned only zeros
on a WebGPU graphics card for tiles of 256 pixels and more, while it was right
on the processor. The float32 file computes the same on both and is not
slower. The conversion script is FerrumPix's
packaging/Diagnostics/TreeNetMessung/nach_fp32.py.

Training data
-------------
The models were trained on RawNIND, the Raw Natural Image Noise Dataset
(Benoit Brummer and Christophe De Vleeschouwer, UCLouvain):

  https://doi.org/10.14428/DVN/DEQCIM

RawNIND is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International (CC BY-SA 4.0); the license text is in
LICENSE.CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt in this folder. It is named here as the source of the
training data. Whether the ShareAlike condition extends to weights trained on
the data is not settled; these weights are therefore not placed under any
license other than the one they were published with.

Model contract (the same for both files)
--------------
  Input   "input"   float32 [batch, 3, H, W]  linear Rec.2020 RGB from the raw
                    file after white balance and demosaicing, white at 1,
                    clipped to 0..1
  Input   "cond"    float32 [batch, 1]        ISO / 6400
  Output  "output"  float32 [batch, 3, H, W]  linear Rec.2020 RGB

H and W are free; FerrumPix uses tiles of 512 (256 on cards with less than
4 GB), overlapping by 32 pixels and blended linearly across the overlap.
There is no strength parameter beyond the ISO; FerrumPix blends the result
against the input in brightness only.

FerrumPix uses it for Bayer and for Fujifilm X-Trans sensors: it gets the
data after demosaicing, and on X-Trans files it measured as well as on Bayer
files. RawForge's separate X-Trans models are not part of this collection.
